# JobPulse PRO — 1-Click Chrome Extension ⚡

Automatically capture job applications from **LinkedIn, Indeed, Greenhouse, Lever, and Naukri** directly into your JobPulse dashboard with a single click.

---

## 🚀 How to Install in Google Chrome (Takes 20 Seconds):

1. Open Google Chrome and type: `chrome://extensions` in the URL bar.
2. Toggle on **"Developer mode"** in the top right corner.
3. Click **"Load unpacked"** in the top left corner.
4. Select this directory:
   `/Users/yashtailor/Documents/Projects/job-tracker/extension`
5. That's it! 🎉

---

## 🎯 How to Use:
1. Open any job posting on **LinkedIn**, **Indeed**, or **Greenhouse**.
2. Notice the floating **"✨ Save to JobPulse"** badge at the bottom-right of your screen.
3. Click it — the company name, job title, location, and link are automatically saved to your JobPulse Pipeline Board!
